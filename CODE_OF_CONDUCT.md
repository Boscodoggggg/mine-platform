# Code of Conduct / 社区行为准则

## Our commitment

We are committed to making mine-platform a professional, welcoming and technically rigorous community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, employer or geographic location.

Industrial and mine-safety systems benefit from direct technical disagreement, but disagreement must stay focused on evidence, risk and design. Personal attacks, intimidation and disclosure of private field information are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and specific.
- Critique code, contracts and evidence rather than people or organizations.
- State assumptions, safety boundaries and uncertainty honestly.
- Accept correction and help turn feedback into reproducible tests or documentation.
- Protect credentials, personal data, customer names, proprietary protocols and sensitive mine information.
- Give credit for ideas, reports, code and field evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or deliberate humiliation.
- Trolling, repeated disruption, bad-faith argument or pressuring maintainers to bypass safety gates.
- Publishing private communication, credentials, personal data, customer material or vulnerability details without permission.
- Impersonation, plagiarism, spam, undisclosed promotion or manipulation of project popularity.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a defect, safety concern or conduct issue in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions, issue warnings or temporarily/permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately and avoid exposing reporters.

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method listed on the [repository owner's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Boscodoggggg). Security vulnerabilities must instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical; conflicts of interest should be disclosed so another maintainer can review the case.
